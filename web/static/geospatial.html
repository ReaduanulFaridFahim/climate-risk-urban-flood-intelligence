Scientific Transition Finding

Under SSP5-8.5 (2041–2070), Bangladesh experiences massive north-western expansion of Tropical Monsoon (Am) biomes, replacing historical Temperate Dry Winter (Cwa) regions in Rangpur and Rajshahi divisions. This drives prolonged heat stress and shifts rainfall distributions into erratic, high-intensity deluge events.
