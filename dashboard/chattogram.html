🌊 Dynamic Drainage Congestion Mechanics

Chattogram suffers from a complex compound hazard mechanism:

  • Tidal Backwater Lock: High tides in the Bay of Bengal push sea water up the Karnafuli River and Chaktai canal network, preventing gravitational drainage.
  • Hill Escarpment Runoff: Heavy monsoon rains over the surrounding hills cascade down steep gradients into low-lying urban wards within minutes.
  • Siltation & Solid Waste: Chaktai, Mirza, and Shitalakshya khal siltation reduces stormwater carrying capacity by over 60%.
Priority Intervention Wards: Chandgaon, Mohra, Bakalia, Panchlaish, and Halishahar.
